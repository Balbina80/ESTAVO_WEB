FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to evaluate Estavo.

Can you migrate our existing data?

Yes. Basic migration can include properties, contacts and active tenancy information. The final scope and quality checks are agreed during onboarding.

Is there a long-term contract?

Commercial terms will be confirmed in the customer agreement. Founding client offers are expected to use flexible initial terms.

Can we add more users later?

Yes. Plans are designed to scale, and additional users can be added where required.

Does Estavo include training?

Guided onboarding is available. Additional training sessions can be included in a tailored proposal.

Is Estavo available on mobile?

The web application is being built responsively. A dedicated native mobile app is not currently promised.

Does Estavo include AI?

AI-assisted workflows are part of the product direction. Availability and usage limits will depend on the selected plan and launch readiness.

Can we cancel or change plan?

Plan changes and cancellation terms will be set out clearly in the final subscription agreement.

How long does onboarding take?

Timing depends on the number of users, data quality and migration scope. A realistic timetable is agreed before work begins.
